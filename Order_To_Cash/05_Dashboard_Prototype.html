The source now supplies the rate

Tested, not inferred
Exchange_Rate is exactThe v2 query brings back Exchange_Date, Exchange_Rate and Exchange_Rate_Type from Sales_Order_V. Exchange_Rate reproduces the USD price exactly on 100% of 25,834 rows, and the list price on 100% of 23,602. When the source has done the arithmetic there is no reason to redo it.
Exchange_Date is the shipment dateIt equals Line_Actual_Shipment_Date on 100% of the 28,719 shipped rows carrying one — zero offset. The earlier inference pointed at the same date; the source now confirms it directly.
Corporate confirmedExchange_Rate_Type reads 'Corporate' on every populated row, which independently confirms the conversion type chosen for the rate dimension. Of 55 types in d_exchange_rate, Corporate is the only one with complete daily coverage.

On-time shipping against three baselines

Which one you report determines whether service looks acceptable or poor
A promising problem, not a speed problemCycle time held between 23 and 30 days all year with no trend, yet on-time against promise fell to 39.8% in May. Operations did not get slower — the promises got more optimistic. Set promise dates from the 66-day P90, not the best case.

Distribution across all 58,912 shipped lines
The tail matters more than the average40% ship on time or early. But 1,637 lines shipped more than 30 days late — small in count, and exactly the kind of failure that costs a relationship. Median delay is 0.3 days early; the mean is dragged to 14.9 days late entirely by this tail.

Ordered to shipped to invoiced

The view the bridge architecture exists to make possible
Billing is not the problemOnce volume ships, 100.0% of it gets invoiced — essentially no leakage between shipping and billing, which is unusual and worth protecting. The entire gap is in fulfilment: 19.7% of ordered volume has not shipped, sitting as 4.41m tonnes across 5,423 open lines.

Model health

Corrections applied and guards in place
Four corrections applied at load48 malformed shipment rows removed (else tonnage reads 13x high) · return signs normalised (else volume +4%) · sentinel dates capped at 2030 (else axes reach 2400) · non-breaking spaces stripped (else 13 return reasons look like 15).
One source defect outstanding0 cancelled lines are present despite the query filtering them. The WHERE clause in all three source queries has an operator-precedence bug — AND binds before OR, so the cancelled filter is bypassed whenever the second date branch is true. One pair of brackets fixes it.
The guard that mattersInvoiced MT must never exceed Ordered MT at the grand-total level. If it does, a relationship has been added between two fact tables and every number is inflated. The Fan Out Alert measure sits on the canvas for exactly this.
